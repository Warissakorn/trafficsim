## The rule the owner chose, and the one measured and rejected

The owner first chose `comfortableDeceleration` (2 m/s² for a car) as "cannot stop".

Measured on seed 42, it **broke the gate NEXT set**, zero major-road clamps:
- **T-junction congested variant:** a minor driver committed about 2 s ahead of an eastbound
  vehicle. The eastbound vehicle was then held at the crossing entry, clamped 0.4 m short at
  7.5 m/s.
- **M2.6 template:** at a derived merge, a turn committed in front of a through vehicle within
  headway of the join. Through vehicles were clamped standing at the join (36 clamps, against
  28).

The owner then chose `maxDeceleration` (8 m/s² for a car). That is what is measured below.

## What the rows show

**Safety clamps fell:**
- **Gap arm** (12 runs, gapTime 3/5/7 s): **76 → 3**. The three left are minor vehicles at
  0–0.5 m/s within 0.1 m of their line.
- **Headway runs** of the free-flow sweep (8 runs): **52 → 0**.
- **Congested headway arm** (12 runs): **83 → 20**.
  - 15 are eastbound vehicles at the fixed-time head, 0–0.6 m short of it: the amber-as-red
    clamp of D36, not right-of-way.
  - 5 are minor vehicles standing or at walking pace within 1 m of the merge line. Their stopping
    distance is about zero, so they are not the case commitment addresses. Their cause is **not
    yet diagnosed** (NEXT).
- **No clamp anywhere is a major vehicle at a conflict area.**
  `tjunction.commitment_removes_the_minor_clamps_*` asserts this for the four seeds at the base
  rule.

**Everything drained:** all 32 runs have active = pending = 0.

**Minor delay fell, as expected.** A committed driver no longer stops for a gap that closed
under it. The change is between 0 and 11 s per movement.
- Gap arm, crossing turn at gapTime 5 s: seed 0 46.5 → 45.7 s, seed 42 21.4 → 19.2 s, seed 43
  20.7 → 17.6 s, seed 4294967295 47.1 → 44.4 s.
- The largest change is gapTime 7 s, seed 42: 54.8 → 44.0 s.

**The gapTime response still holds.** For every seed:
- both minor movements' delay rose strictly from 3 to 5 to 7 s;
- the minor queue's mean and maximum rose strictly too (seed 42 max: 27.64 / 27.69 / 34.54 m).

**The congested headway arm still responds to headway.**
- Near turn, headway 3 / 7 / 12 m, in every seed: seed 0 84.6 / 86.7 / 87.4 s, seed 42
  29.1 / 31.5 / 38.1 s, seed 43 30.7 / 36.6 / 61.6 s, seed 4294967295 54.9 / 56.2 / 58.3 s.
- Crossing turn: seeds 42 and 43 respond (44.9 / 51.4 / 51.4 s; 55.9 / 58.6 / 63.9 s). Seeds 0
  and 4294967295 are flat, as before.
