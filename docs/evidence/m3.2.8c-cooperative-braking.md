## What this shows

- **D89's measure moves the right way.** East → South dead-end wait seconds fall by 59%, and all
  dead-end waits by 53%. East → West falls by 2.3 ± 0.6 s against 8b, which leaves +3.7 ± 1.5 s
  against `b472e05`. D89's slope (0.047 s per wait-second) predicts a 3.5 s fall from 75 s fewer
  waits. 2.3 ± 0.6 s is about two thirds of that. **D89's failure condition did not fire**: the
  waits fell and East → West fell with them.
- **About half of M3.2.8b's network cost is recovered.** The mean is +2.4 → +1.3 ± 0.5 s against
  `b472e05`, and lower than 8b in every comparison where it moved. Clamps did not rise
  (−0.8 ± 0.5).
- **Waits remain.** 43 East → South vehicles still wait, up to 90 s. The candidates are a stream
  that cannot be slowed at 3 m/s², a target lane standing at a red with no room, and a changer
  that stops behind its own lane's queue before its look-ahead begins. None is separated here.
- North → West's +2.7 ± 1.2 s does not move. It was never linked to the waits (step 5).

## Not shown

- Why 43 East → South waits remain: the stream at a red, its own queue, or too little room at
  3 m/s².
- Sensitivity to `maxDecelerationCooperativeBraking` (only 3 m/s² was run) and to the look-ahead
  definition.
- The courtesy-hold time, which no diagnostic counts yet.
- Windows/MSVC runs; the desktop build; callgrind cost.
