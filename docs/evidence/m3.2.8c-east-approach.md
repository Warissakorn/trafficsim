# M3.2.8c step 5: the East-approach rise is the left-turn dead-end waits

Development evidence for `docs/NEXT.md`'s M3.2.8c step 5, after
[`m3.2.8c-arrival-phases.md`](m3.2.8c-arrival-phases.md). Over seeds 42–81 that step found
M3.2.8b's persistent cost on the East approach: through (East → West) +6.0 ± 1.7 s and left
(East → South) +6.8 ± 2.6 s. This step places it. **Not calibration and not M6 validation**; the
not-yet-validated marker stays. No behaviour changed in this session.

## Inputs

- Seeds 42–81, `data/projects/m2.6-study-template.traffic.json`.
- **After:** this branch, `--segment-times --stop-lines --lane-changes`.
- **Before:** `b472e05`. `segment_times` and `stop_lines` were copied into a scratch worktree and
  observed behind environment variables, uncommitted. `--lane-changes` has no "before", since
  there were no lane changes then.
- In all 80 runs, output with the diagnostics on equals output without them, the diagnostics'
  keys apart. **Linux only**: WSL2, GCC 15.2, headless Debug.
- East geometry, from the project file. Vehicles depart on the entry Link, the route's first
  segment, which the left-turn stubs dead-end on (step 1: about 277 m from the edge). They then
  pass `connector-23` and reach `link-16`, where the stop line stands 43 m in. Its lanes are
  `lane-17` (lane 1, `head-65`, through and left), `lane-18` (lane 2, `head-66`, through) and
  `lane-19` (lane 3, `head-67`, right). Left-turners leave by `connector-53`, through traffic by
  `connector-52`.

Δ is after minus before, per seed, as mean ± standard error over 40 seeds.

A per-seed regression of each movement's delay change on its approach's left-turn dead-end wait

| East → South (L) | East → South | 0.47 | 0.059 | −0.7 | +10.2 ± 4.5 | +3.3 ± 2.6 |

At the mean East → South wait of 126.8 s per seed, the fit gives East → West
0.1 + 0.047 × 126.8 = **+6.0 s**, the whole measured rise. With no waits, it predicts no rise.
West is the same mechanism, smaller: 55.2 s of waits predict +1.6 s, against the measured
+1.7 ± 0.7. East → North (lane 3) is not linked to them.

## What this shows

- **M3.2.8b's persistent cost is the left-turn dead-end waits.** A left-turner entering on
  lane 2 or 3 that cannot get into lane 1 stops at its dead end on the entry Link. It holds up
  the vehicles behind it in its lane, and the courtesy rule holds a lane-1 vehicle for it. Seeds
  with long waits carry the rise, and seeds without them show none.
- This is the evidence for the next M3.2.8c row: **cooperation with a deceleration parameter and
  look-ahead**, so a left-turner is let in before it has to stop. That row's measure of success is
  in this file: East → South dead-end wait seconds, and East → West delay against `b472e05`,
  over seeds 42–81.
- `laneChangeDistance` stays unneeded here (D87). The left-turners also change at the first
  tick the rules allow. They wait because no gap is offered, not because they start late.

## Not shown

- How much of the loss is the waiting vehicle blocking its own lane, and how much is the courtesy
  hold in lane 1. Separating them needs a courtesy-hold count, which no diagnostic gives yet.
- Why East's waits are more than twice West's: the volumes and the lane split are the
  candidates, and they were not measured.
- East → North's +3.1 ± 1.5 s and North → West's +2.7 ± 1.2 s, which are not linked to the waits.
- Windows/MSVC runs; the desktop build.
