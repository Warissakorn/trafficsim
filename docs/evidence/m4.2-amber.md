simulated delays from an unvalidated prototype model, not HCM control delay or LOS.

## Rule measured

[AMBER](../reference/AMBER.md):
- **Amber:** a vehicle goes when `speed² > 2 · 3.0 · gap`.
- **Red:** it goes only when it cannot stop at its type's maximum deceleration.
- **Data:** the catalog behaviour carries `amberDeceleration: 3`.
