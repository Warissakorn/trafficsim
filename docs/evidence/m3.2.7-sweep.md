# M3.2.7b — T-junction diagnostic sweep

Development evidence for A26 (`docs/M3_ACCEPTANCE.md` §2). **Not calibration and not M6
validation:** one unvalidated prototype car-following model, round-number demand, and
test inputs, not calibrated values. The not-yet-validated marker stays.

- **Inputs:** fixed in `tools/t_junction_sweep.hpp` and committed as
  [`m3.2.7-sweep-metadata.json`](m3.2.7-sweep-metadata.json) (commit `b66313b`) before any row was
  produced. `tjunction.the_archived_sweep_metadata_still_describes_the_fixture` fails if the
  fixture or a catalog changes.
- **Rows:** [`m3.2.7-sweep.csv`](m3.2.7-sweep.csv), from
  `trafficsim-t-junction-sweep --run`, the right-hand Yield base, on Linux with GCC (the build
  line is in the metadata).
- **Per run:** completed trips and whole-route mean delay per movement, the minor approach queue
  (mean and maximum), active, pending and safety clamps.

## What the rows show

- **Everything was served.** Every run drained: active = pending = 0 in all 20 runs. So no mean
  below is flattered by vehicles left unserved.
- **gapTime 3 → 5 → 7 s at headway 7 m.**
  - Both minor movements' mean delay and the minor queue rose at each step, for every one of the
    four seeds.
  - The per-seed spread is large: gapTime 5 s gives the crossing turn 20.7–47.1 s. This is one
    run per seed at low minor volume (19–33 trips per movement). It is not a claim that delay is
    monotone in gapTime for every seed, which §2 explicitly does not require.
  - The major movements' delay barely moved (3.6–4.6 s): the major road does not yield.
- **headway 3 / 7 / 12 m at gapTime 5 s: identical rows.** Investigated, not assumed:
  - Over all four seeds at headway 12 m, no tick had a major vehicle within 12 m of an entry that
    was outside the gap-time window.
  - The slowest major vehicle seen within 12 m of an entry was 10.1 m/s, so its time to entry was
    at most 1.2 s, well inside 5 s. Headway only decides a block when a major vehicle is slower
    than headway / gapTime (2.4 m/s at 12 m).
  - **At this demand the headway arm exercises nothing.** Headway is covered by the controlled
    predicate test (`tjunction_controlled.the_headway_threshold_*`). A congested major-road
    variant would be needed to exercise it end to end; that is left to M3.2.7c.
- **Safety clamps: 3–10 per run.** In the gap arm all 60 fell on minor vehicles within 1.5 m of a
  waiting line, and none on the major road. A line closes the tick a major vehicle enters the
  gap-time window, and a minor driver already too close to stop is clamped: there is no
  commitment rule. This is carved into M3.2.8 (D66); nothing was tuned here.

No event traces are archived. The one unexplained-looking result (identical headway rows) was
explained by the investigation above, from the model's own predicate, without traces.
