## Diagnosis

The current D80 fixture produces five minor-road clamp events across seeds 42/43
and headways 3/7/12 m at gapTime 5 s. This is a new measurement, not a replay claim
about D69's geometry. The archived pre-D80 document fails today's coverage validation
(`CONFLICT_EXTENT_UNCOVERED`); its evidence and metadata are retained unchanged.

Exact traces, including the unrelated major-road amber clamps:
[before](m3.2.8a-clamps-before.jsonl), [after](m3.2.8a-clamps-after.jsonl).

All five are limited by the vehicle leader's **standstill gap**, not commitment or
the phase-2 swept check. Once a major front enters the shared segment, its rear is
clipped to that segment's origin until the body joins it. The minor sees this span
through its downstream shared segment. The leader gap is below the 2 m
`standstillDistance`, making the hard movement allowance zero, but the outgoing
leader's speed makes following propose +2.5 m/s². A stopped minor proposes 0.0125 m;
the cap immediately cuts it to zero and reports an emergency clamp.

D105 prevents positive following acceleration at exactly zero speed when the leader
gap is at or below `standstillDistance`. It waits before integration, then releases
as soon as room opens. It changes no movement cap, buffer, commitment, admission or
emergency-event reporting. No tolerance or new state; ordinary stopped followers
use the same rule.

The moving case requires 9.055773 m/s² to stop from 0.905577 m/s in one 0.1 s tick,
above the car's 8 m/s² maximum, with zero permitted movement. It remains counted.
Its actual rear gap is positive: the conservative standstill-buffer cap is the
constraint, not vehicle-body overlap. Its abrupt onset needs an anticipation/motion
contract (M3.2.8a.1).

  counterpart passes before and after, preserving real emergency reporting. Three
  `core` tests cover stationary wait/release, moving clamp and the exact boundary.

  setting `TMPDIR` to a writable workspace directory resolved three environment
  failures without a product change. Frozen baselines were not regenerated.

  copied replay, generated = completed + active + pending, shared-segment body

## Reproduce

Configure/build headless (or desktop), then:

```bash
./build/headless/bin/trafficsim-t-junction-clamps --trace after.jsonl .
./build/headless/bin/trafficsim-t-junction-clamps --sweep after-sweep.jsonl .
./build/headless/bin/trafficsim-t-junction-clamps --trace-all all-after.jsonl .
ctest --preset headless
cmake --build build/headless --target check
```

For before, use `f538a7e` in a scratch worktree, copy only the diagnostic source and
its CMake target stanza, and build with the same compiler/options/dependencies.
This session linked the diagnostic to the preserved baseline core; model, project,
evaluation and commands were unchanged. For project comparisons, run each CLI with
`--project <file> --data-dir data` for seeds 42–81 and compare all JSON fields, excluding
only `safetyClamps`.
