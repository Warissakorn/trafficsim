# M3.2.8c step 2: where the South/North right-turn delay grows

> **Read with [`m3.2.8c-arrival-phases.md`](m3.2.8c-arrival-phases.md) (2026-10-01, D88).** Over 40
> seeds the South/North right-turn rise measured here is not M3.2.8b's effect: seeds 42–46 drew
> differently timed departures before and after. The measurements stand; that reading does not.

Development evidence for `docs/NEXT.md`'s M3.2.8c step 2. It follows
[`m3.2.8c-right-turns.md`](m3.2.8c-right-turns.md), which found the South → East and
North → West right-turn delay up in every seed after M3.2.8b, with no dead-end waits behind it.
**Not calibration and not M6 validation**; the not-yet-validated marker stays. No behaviour
changed in this session.

## Inputs

- `trafficsim-cli N --project data/projects/m2.6-study-template.traffic.json --segment-times`,
  seeds 42–46. The diagnostic is `SegmentTimeAccumulator` (`src/eval/segment_times.hpp`): per
  movement a vehicle arrived on, the mean time from its departure (insertion) to entering each
  runtime segment, plus the arrival's departure delay, travel time and free-flow time.
- **Before:** the engine at `b472e05`, with the diagnostic's two files and a small CLI patch
  copied into a scratch worktree, uncommitted. The diagnostic reads only events that predate
  M3.2.8b. **After:** this branch.
- Both binaries' default output was `cmp`-identical to their earlier runs, so the copied
  diagnostic changed nothing. After = `d046c2c` (M3.2.8b) byte for byte, as step 1 checked.
- **Linux only**: WSL2 Ubuntu, GCC 15.2, headless Debug.
- Segment ids are lane ids, labelled here from the project file. South: approach `lane-26/27`
  (Link 25), pocket entry `connector-36`, right-turn pocket `lane-31`, then `connector-57` to
  the East exit (`lane-22`). North: `connector-48`, pocket `lane-43`, `connector-60`, and the
  West exit (`lane-10`). `connector-57` and `connector-60` start at the pocket's stop line.

## What this shows

- **The whole right-turn rise is spent in the pocket, before its stop line.** It is not the
  insertion (departure delay unchanged), not the entry Link (pocket reached within 0.5 s of
  before), and not the junction or the exit (no growth after the stop line).
- **Correction to step 1.** Step 1 named "gap acceptance against the opposing stream" as a
  candidate. The M2.6 timing plan is split-phase: South 0–24 s, East 28–54 s, North 58–79 s,
  West 83–116 s, each approach alone. A right-turner at its own green meets no opposing through
  stream, so that candidate does not apply here.
- What remains is time held at the pocket's own signal. A vehicle that misses its approach's
  green waits most of a cycle, so +7.5 s on average means only a few percent more right-turners
  miss a green. Why more of them miss it is not shown. The pocket is reached as early as before.
  Its queue grew (8b evidence: South pocket mean 15.1 → 18.8 m), so discharge through the stop
  line is the next place to look.
