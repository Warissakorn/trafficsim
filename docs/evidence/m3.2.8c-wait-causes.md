# M3.2.8c — why the remaining dead-end waits wait (D92)

Development evidence for NEXT.md's M3.2.8c option 1, after cooperative braking (D90,
[`m3.2.8c-cooperative-braking.md`](m3.2.8c-cooperative-braking.md)). A measurement: no engine
behaviour changed. **Not calibration and not M6 validation**: the same unvalidated car-following
model, and the not-yet-validated marker stays.

## Inputs

- `data/projects/m2.6-study-template.traffic.json`, seeds 42–81, `--lane-changes --wait-causes`.
- `maxDecelerationCooperativeBraking` 2, 3 and 4 m/s²: copies of `data/` that differ only in
  `driver-behaviour/default.json`, run with `--data-dir`. 3 is the shipped default.
- **Windows only**: MSVC 19.51 (VS 18), Release. No Linux run.
- **Toolchain check:** at 3 m/s² every number shared with the GCC 15.2 evidence of D90
  matches: East → South 43 waiting vehicles and 2,067 s, all movements 4,222 s, network mean
  51.1 s, East → West 63.8 s, clamps 23.7 per seed, and seed 42's 48.94 s and 20 clamps.

## Causes

`--wait-causes` (`src/eval/dead_end_waits.hpp`) decides each wait's cause once, from the
snapshot that starts it, in this order:

1. **noMovingApproach**: on this route the vehicle never moved at walking pace or faster inside
   a span and inside its look-ahead (`deadEndGoverns`), so cooperative braking never applied.
2. **outsideSpan**: no span target at its place.
3. **targetStanding**: at its mapped place on the target lane, a vehicle alongside it or the
   nearest one behind is below walking pace (a red or a queue).
4. **movingStream**: otherwise. The target lane moves, and nobody fell in behind.

A wait is `waitingAtDeadEnd`, exactly as `--lane-changes` counts it. Wait seconds reconcile with
`--lane-changes` for every movement at every setting.

**A first definition did not discriminate.** NEXT.md's wording, "stopped behind its own lane's
queue before its look-ahead began", was tried first as "stood (below walking pace) on this route
while its dead end did not yet govern". It classified **all 118 waits** (4,222 s) at 3 m/s² that
way. With the definition above, **none** of them falls under it. Every waiting vehicle had stood
in its own lane's queue earlier, and every one later moved inside its look-ahead. Standing in
its own queue is common to all of them, so it is not a cause that separates one wait from
another. The test
`standing_in_its_own_lanes_queue_first_does_not_make_it_no_moving_approach` holds this.

## 1. Waits by cause, 3 m/s², 40 seeds pooled (waits / seconds)

| Movement | Waiting vehicles | Wait (s) | targetStanding | movingStream |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| East → South (L) | 43 | 2,067 | **36 / 1,925** | 7 / 142 |
| West → North (L) | 37 | 1,183 | 29 / 1,099 | 8 / 84 |
| North → East (L) | 10 | 432 | 10 / 432 | 0 / 0 |
| South → West (L) | 10 | 336 | 9 / 263 | 1 / 73 |
| East → North (R) | 12 | 139 | 8 / 130 | 4 / 8 |
| West → South (R) | 5 | 31 | 3 / 30 | 2 / 1 |
| unfinished | 1 | 34 | 1 / 34 | 0 / 0 |
| **All** | 118 | 4,222 | **96 / 3,913 (93%)** | 22 / 309 (7%) |

noMovingApproach and outsideSpan are 0 at every setting.

**Reading:** 93% of the remaining wait time starts beside a target lane that is standing. A
standing helper cannot brake to make room, so no cooperation rule reaches these waits. They are
the target lane's red or queue, and the changer waits until it moves. The 7% beside a moving
stream is what cooperation could still act on.

## 2. Sensitivity to `maxDecelerationCooperativeBraking`

Per seed, mean over 40 seeds. Δ is the per-seed difference against 3, as mean ± standard error.

| Metric | 2 | 3 | 4 | 2 − 3 | 4 − 3 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Dead-end wait (s), East → South | 57.0 | 51.7 | 47.4 | +5.3 ± 4.3 | −4.3 ± 3.0 |
| Dead-end wait (s), all | 112.6 | 105.5 | 101.2 | +7.0 ± 4.5 | −4.4 ± 3.0 |
| East → West delay (s) | 64.9 | 63.8 | 63.7 | +1.1 ± 0.9 | −0.1 ± 0.1 |
| East → South delay (s) | 59.2 | 57.2 | 56.8 | +2.0 ± 1.6 | −0.4 ± 0.3 |
| Network mean delay (s) | 51.5 | 51.1 | 51.1 | +0.4 ± 0.3 | −0.1 ± 0.0 |
| Safety clamps | 23.4 | 23.7 | 23.4 | −0.3 ± 0.4 | −0.3 ± 0.2 |

The movingStream share moves with the parameter (East → South 238 → 142 → 52 s; all
405 → 309 → 216 s), while targetStanding barely moves (4,097 → 3,913 → 3,831 s). No difference
reaches two standard errors except network mean at 4 (−0.1 s, standard error under 0.05). Clamps do not rise. The
parameter acts only on the 7% it can reach. The default stays 3 (no owner ruling).

## Reproduce

Build the Release `trafficsim-cli`. For each value, copy `data/` and set the field, then run:

```
trafficsim-cli --seed N --data-dir DATA --project data/projects/m2.6-study-template.traffic.json --lane-changes --wait-causes
```

for N = 42…81. Pool `waitCauses.rows[].causes` and `laneChangeDiagnostics.rows[].waitSeconds`
per movement.
